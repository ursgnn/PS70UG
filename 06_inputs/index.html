Building a Capacitive Sensor

For the first part of our assignment we were fortunate enough to do it in groups at the end of class. Our group's assignment was to build a water level sensor using capacitors. We decided to use an empty water bottle and fix to capacitors so they would face each other and our idea worked pretty good!

Another Sensor

For my second sensor project I decided to use a photoresistor to measure the light in a room(a box). For my box I found an amazing website filled with many laser cutter templates and I found a slider box template that I decided to print for my sensor.

This is the website I was talking about.

As always I had a major problem with my printing again. I didn't change the thickness measure on the website so when I printed my box that was designed to be printed on a 3mm cardboard on a 6mm cardboard, it became really hard to construct. After an hour of reprinting and lots of hammering I made it work. (DISCLAIMER: I put ducktape over the handle to maximize the darkness before I recorded my data.)
